Advanced experimental technology is the backbone of earthquake engineering research. My work in this area spans:

- **Shake table control** — control frameworks for uniaxial and 6-DOF shake tables that balance tracking performance and robustness, developed at NCREE and NTU.
- **Floor response reproduction** — passive transfer systems and advanced control strategies that allow a shake table to reproduce the floor motions of high-rise buildings, enabling realistic testing of nonstructural components.
- **System identification** — kinematic and dynamic identification of multi-axis shake table systems from actuator measurements.
- **Laboratory software** — toolkits for signal processing, data conversion, and test automation (dSPACEinPython, STEX2BIN, TriGen, SigGen).
