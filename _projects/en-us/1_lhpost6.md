The NHERI@UCSD Large High-Performance Outdoor Shake Table (LHPOST6) is the world's largest outdoor shake table, capable of reproducing 6-DOF ground motions on full-scale structures. My doctoral research developed a comprehensive nonlinear dynamic model — a digital twin — of this facility.

The model captures the platen rigid-body dynamics using twists and wrenches, servo-hydraulic dynamics, pneumatic hold-down struts governed by the ideal gas law, and nonlinear dissipative forces. It was calibrated and validated against experimental data under both bare and loaded table conditions, including tests with the 10-story TallWood mass-timber building specimen.

Ongoing work uses this digital twin to study control–table–specimen dynamic interaction and to improve acceleration tracking performance through fully numerical iteration strategies.
