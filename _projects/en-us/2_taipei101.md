Taipei 101 houses one of the world's most famous tuned mass dampers — a 660-tonne steel sphere suspended near the top of the tower. This project, funded by the National Science and Technology Council (Taiwan), investigates how active control technology can enhance the damper system.

The research aims to suppress damper displacement under extreme wind and seismic events while improving the overall energy dissipation of the system, combining structural control theory, system identification, and experimental validation.
